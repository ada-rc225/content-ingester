The lesson was reworked for second-year mechanical engineering students by replacing the abstract linear-algebra framing with structural vibration, modal analysis, and the dominant vibration mode. The core mathematical definitions, assumptions, update rule, Rayleigh quotient, residual criterion, and failure boundaries were preserved, while the explanation was reorganised to make the numerical method feel relevant to engineering practice. A compact executable NumPy example was included and linked directly to the algorithmic steps so students can see how the mathematics maps to code.
