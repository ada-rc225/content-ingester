The lesson was reworked from a generic Newton's method note into a second-year mechanical engineering narrative centered on nonlinear spring equilibrium and force balance. The adaptation preserves the source definitions, equations, local convergence conditions, stopping criteria, damping rule, and failure boundaries while replacing the abstract setting with a familiar mechanical example. The structure uses six compact sections with explicit chapter headings, a short executable Python example, and six exercises that connect the mathematics to code and engineering interpretation. The main limitation is that the lesson remains a compact introduction rather than a full treatment of nonlinear solvers, so it prioritises readability and conceptual linkage over deeper numerical analysis.
