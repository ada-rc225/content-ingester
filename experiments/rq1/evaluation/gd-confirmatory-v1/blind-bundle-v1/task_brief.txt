The learners have studied calculus, vectors, matrices, and basic Python, but have not studied formal convex optimisation.

- smoothness, convexity, and strong convexity;

- convergence results for convex and strongly convex objectives;
- Heavy Ball and Nesterov acceleration;
- stochastic-gradient assumptions and step-size conditions;
- AdaGrad, RMSProp, and Adam;
- Newton and BFGS methods;
- the connection between mathematical updates and Python implementation.

2. one hand-calculation exercise involving an objective, its gradient,

3. one Python code-diagnostic exercise containing buggy and corrected code.
